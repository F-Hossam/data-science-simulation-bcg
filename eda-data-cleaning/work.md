# Task
- investigate whether price sensitivity is the most influential factor
- analyze the following using Python:
    - The data types of each column
    - Distributions of columns

# Key notes
- Price sensitivity is the degree to which customer behaviors change in response to a change in price
- “demand” refers to the demand for energy consumption.
- Sensitivity is [% change in quantity demanded / % change in price]
    - greater than 1: the price is very likely to influence how much people buy

# Datasets
- Historical customer data: Customer data such as usage, sign up date, forecasted usage etc
- Historical pricing data: variable and fixed pricing data at different points in time
- Churn indicator: whether each customer has churned or not
